How commercial links and advertising are handled.

Some outbound links on RateScope may be affiliate links. If you click one and make a qualifying purchase, RateScope may earn a commission at no additional cost to you.

Editorial independence

A commercial relationship does not buy a positive review, a calculator result, or an unsupported claim. Recommendations should be relevant to the reader and labeled where required.

Advertising

Display advertising may appear on some pages. Advertisements are separated from editorial content and may be selected by a third-party advertising provider. An advertisement does not imply endorsement by RateScope.

How to identify commercial links

Affiliate links use a sponsored relationship and may open in a new tab. Relevant pages include a disclosure before or near the link. If you prefer not to use affiliate links, you can usually visit the provider directly.
