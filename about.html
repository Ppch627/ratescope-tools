About RateScope

Clearer pricing decisions for independent work.

RateScope is a small library of free calculators and plain-English guides for freelancers, consultants, and solo service businesses.

Why this site exists

Many freelance pricing discussions start with a market average and end with a discount. That approach ignores taxes, downtime, software, sales, administration, and the simple fact that independent professionals cannot bill every working hour.

RateScope starts from business math. Each calculator makes its assumptions visible so you can challenge the inputs and use the result as a decision tool rather than a magic answer.

How the calculators work

All calculation happens locally in your browser. The site does not need an account and does not send calculator inputs to a RateScope database. Results are planning estimates and may not include every tax, legal, financing, or operating cost that applies to your situation.

Editorial approach

Guides favor repeatable processes over aggressive tactics. They avoid promising a specific income, conversion rate, or market response. Pricing differs by location, industry, skill, demand, client type, and business model, so context matters.

How the site can be sustained

If advertising, affiliate partnerships, or paid products are enabled, they will be disclosed clearly. Commercial relationships should never change a calculator's formula or turn editorial guidance into a disguised advertisement.
