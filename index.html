    Common questions

    Straight answers

    Are the calculators free?

    Yes. The tools run in your browser and do not require an account. The site may be supported by advertising or affiliate links in the future; that will never change the calculation.

    Do these results replace legal or tax advice?

    No. They are planning estimates. Tax rules, contract terms, interest limits, and payment practices vary by location, so confirm important decisions with a qualified professional.

    Should I charge hourly or by project?

    Use an hourly rate as your internal cost model, even when you quote a fixed price. The client can buy an outcome while you still know whether the outcome is profitable.

    What if my market will not pay the calculated rate?

    Change the business model, not just the number. Narrow your offer, reduce delivery cost, increase utilization, sell a productized service, or target clients who value the specific outcome more highly.
